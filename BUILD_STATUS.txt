Important trade-off:
- Production flush barrier calls MinecraftServer.saveEverything(true,true,true),
  which synchronously flushes loaded world chunks. It may pause a large server.
- This avoids WAL terminal state getting ahead of chunk persistence but does not
  create a single atomic transaction across custom WAL and Minecraft region files.
