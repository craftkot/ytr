- NO durable APPLYING/COMMITTED/ROLLING_BACK phase transitions.
- NO block entity NBT relocation, entity transport, redstone/physics checks, chunk coordination.
- An AFTER match cannot prove Operator executed an operation; automatic restore would be unsafe.
