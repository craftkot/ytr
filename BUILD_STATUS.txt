Implemented in 0.8.0:
- Bounded per-player placement history, confirmed against actual next-tick world state.
- Dimension-aware six-neighbour connected-component construction analysis.
- Structure threshold: 8 connected placed blocks.
- Building-candidate threshold: 24+ blocks and minimum 3x3x3 bounds.
- Per-player construction map cap: 8,192 blocks.
- Removal invalidation when a tracked block is broken by any player, including creative mode.
- Loaded-chunk construction audit of up to 64 blocks per tick; unloaded chunks are never force-loaded.
- Construction analysis budget: one dirty player profile per server tick, with a 20-tick per-profile throttle.
- Persisted construction summary and schema 5 -> 6 migration.
- /operator structures diagnostics and construction metrics in profile/status.
- Builder-aware HOME_ECHO shadow plan.
- World mutations remain disabled.

Known limitations:
- Only ordinary BlockItem placement confirmed through UseBlockCallback is tracked.
- Commands, WorldEdit, world generation and some modded placement paths are not captured.
- Building classification is geometric and heuristic; it does not prove a component is a house.
- Interiors, block states, block-entity NBT and container contents are not stored.
- No actual house relocation, reconstruction or rollback is implemented.
