Implemented in 0.3.0:
- Bounded per-player map of 4,096 unique mined terrain voxels.
- Terrain material classification.
- Six-face connected-component analysis.
- Dimension-aware tunnel reconstruction.
- Preliminary linear/chamber/irregular/small area classification.
- Largest component bounding coordinates.
- /operator tunnels for the current dimension.
- Schema migration from 2 to 3, preserving existing player profiles.
- Chronological eviction order restored after JSON load.

Known limitations:
- Only suitable blocks mined by a player after installing 0.3 are mapped.
- Existing tunnels and air spaces are not scanned.
- Placed blocks, explosions and changes by other mods are not removed from the mined-voxel map.
- The map stores the 4,096 most recently mined unique positions per player across dimensions.
- Geometry classes are heuristics, not a full skeleton/branch reconstruction.
- World mutation is intentionally disabled. Operator does not yet create, extend or alter tunnels.
