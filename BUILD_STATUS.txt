Implemented in 0.6.0:
- Bounded nearby scan for pre-existing connected underground air.
- Scan trigger uses full-collision neighbours and local no-leaves heightmap depth, allowing both narrow tunnels and large underground chambers to be discovered.
- Flood-fill bounds: 12 blocks in each horizontal direction, 6 below and 3 above the player's feet position.
- Up to 4,096 visited positions per scan.
- Maximum 16 concurrent sessions and 128 block checks per server tick globally.
- Scanner only inspects already loaded chunks and never force-loads terrain.
- Explicit cell provenance: mined vs observed_air.
- Scanned air never overwrites mined evidence and never increments mined-block totals.
- When memory is full, old scanned-air cells are evicted before mined cells; a full mined-only map blocks new scanned-air entries.
- Status and player/tunnel reports distinguish mined cells from locally discovered air.
- World-memory schema migrated from 3 to 4; legacy cells without source are treated as mined.
- Removed duplicate /operator network2d registration.
- Height-aware 3D network analysis and 2D compatibility command retained.
- Automatic world mutation remains disabled.

Known limitations:
- This is local discovery around a player, not a full-world or remote-chunk scan.
- Large connected spaces can be truncated by the 4,096-position scan cap.
- Player must be inside or sufficiently below the local surface in an accessible air space for a scan to start.
- Unloaded chunks are skipped without being loaded.
- Explosion-created air is detected only if a player later enters/approaches the affected space and a local scan reaches it; there is no direct explosion event hook yet.
- The scanner records air geometry and does not infer whether a cave is natural or player-built.
- 3D reconstruction remains layer-wise and approximate, not a full 3D medial-axis transform.
- World mutation is intentionally disabled.
