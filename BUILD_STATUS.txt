Implemented:
- Height-aware 3D tunnel graph reconstructed from per-Y centerlines.
- Vertical bands up to three blocks high are treated as normal tunnel clearance.
- Taller vertical runs are retained as shaft segments.
- Stacked passages at different heights remain separate networks.
- 3D dead-end, junction, corridor, vertical-shaft and loop analysis.
- 3D junction coordinates, corridor endpoints and estimated route lengths.
- /operator network now uses the height-aware graph.
- /operator network2d preserves the 0.4 horizontal projection.
- Round-robin audit of up to 128 previously mapped cells per server tick.
- Audit checks only loaded chunks and never force-loads terrain.
- Filled recorded cells are removed from all player profiles.
- World-memory schema remains 3; no migration is required for 0.4 saves.
- World mutation remains disabled.

Known limitations:
- 3D reconstruction is layer-wise: centerlines are computed per Y layer and linked afterward. It is not a full 3D medial-axis transform.
- Vertical bands of up to three blocks are collapsed as tunnel clearance; unusually tall rooms may be represented approximately.
- Only suitable blocks mined after installing the mod are mapped.
- Existing tunnels and air spaces are not scanned.
- Audit can invalidate filled mapped cells but does not discover new air cells created by explosions or other non-player destruction.
- Audit examines loaded chunks only; unloaded chunks are skipped without being loaded.
- Audit budget is 128 mapped cells per server tick across all player profiles.
- World mutation is intentionally disabled.
