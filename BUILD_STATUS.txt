Implemented in 0.9.0:
- Persist serialized BlockState properties per confirmed player-placed block.
- Refresh block state during loaded-chunk audits without counting another placement.
- Floor, roof and four-wall shell coverage estimates.
- Internal horizontal floor-plane detection that excludes the perimeter wall ring.
- Door count (lower half counted once), glass/window blocks, stairs and slabs.
- Enclosed void and unoccupied-cell flood-fill inside the component bounding box, capped at 32,768 cells per component and 65,536 cells per player analysis.
- Semantic geometry labels: ENCLOSED_BUILDING, OPEN_BUILDING, BUILDING_CANDIDATE and STRUCTURE.
- Solid filled volumes and incomplete interior scans are not mislabeled as open buildings.
- Persisted shell and enclosed-volume summary metrics.
- Schema 6 -> 7 migration, preserving construction records and scheduling semantic re-analysis.
- Builder scoring and HOME_ECHO shadow planning now include shell and enclosed-volume evidence.
- Expanded /operator structures diagnostics.
- World mutations remain disabled.

Known limitations:
- Placement capture depends on ordinary BlockItem interactions confirmed on the following tick.
- Enclosed voids are inferred from recorded placements, not a complete world scan.
- Components with bounding-box volume above 32,768 cells, or components exceeding the 65,536-cell profile budget, skip interior flood-fill.
- Block-entity NBT and container contents are not captured.
- No house relocation, reconstruction or rollback is implemented.
