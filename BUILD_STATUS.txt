Implemented in 0.4.0:
- Bounded per-player map of 4,096 unique mined terrain voxels.
- Delayed invalidation when a player fills a recorded mined cell.
- Six-face 3D connected-component reconstruction.
- Preliminary linear/chamber/irregular/small area classification and bounds.
- Horizontal X/Z footprint projection.
- Zhang-Suen centerline thinning with a 128-iteration cap.
- Graph compression into corridor segments and topology nodes.
- Dead-end, junction, isolated-area and loop detection.
- Junction coordinates and approximate corridor segment endpoints.
- /operator network and /operator tunnels commands.
- Existing world memory schema 3 retained; 0.3 saves do not require migration.
- World mutation remains disabled.

Known limitations:
- Network analysis uses horizontal X/Z projection; vertical layers collapse and stacked tunnels may merge.
- Centerline segment lengths are estimates after thinning, not exact block-path lengths.
- Only suitable blocks mined after installing the mod are mapped.
- Existing tunnels and air spaces are not scanned.
- Explosions, pistons, fluid flow, dispensers and other mods' block changes are not fully tracked.
- World mutation is intentionally disabled.
