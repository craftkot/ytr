Implemented in 0.12:
- TransactionJournal full PREPARED source/target before-images, including natural blocks and air.
- TransactionIntegrity deterministic SHA-256 over both before-image regions.
- TransactionJournalStorage: non-overwriting atomic journal persistence, max four/player.
- TransactionPreparationService: valid snapshot + preflight + bounded whole-AABB capture.
- Target AABB must be entirely empty, not only its tracked block positions.
- Explicit /operator transaction prepare x y z and /operator transaction status.
- Pure reverse-preimage planning (no placement or rollback action).
- Unit tests for journal completeness, overlap, duplicate cells, limits and tamper detection.

Limits: 4096 cells per region, 8192 total, 8 MiB journal JSON,
256 KiB NBT per block, 2 MiB total NBT.

Not implemented: world mutation, automatic relocation, rollback execution, crash recovery,
chunk coordination, neighbor/redstone/physics safety and entity relocation.
