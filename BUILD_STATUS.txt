Production durability behavior:
- APPLY terminal COMMITTED is written only after a successful Minecraft world flush.
- ROLLBACK terminal ROLLED_BACK is written only after successful world flush and
  full UNCHANGED preimage verification.
- If the world flush fails, WAL stays in a recoverable nonterminal phase.
- A pending RESTORE intent whose block already matches BEFORE after restart has
  its missing ACK reconciled; if it still matches AFTER, it remains in the retry plan.

Trade-off:
- The current Minecraft adapter uses MinecraftServer.saveEverything(true,true,true).
  This synchronously flushes loaded world chunks and may cause a noticeable pause
  on large servers. It favors safety over save cost for the small manual sandbox.

Not proven:
- Physical power loss, OS/kernel crash, volatile storage-controller cache loss,
  filesystem corruption, or arbitrary write reordering.
- Atomic commit across custom WAL and multiple Anvil region files.
- BlockEntity NBT/inventories, entities, liquids, redstone, modded blocks,
  arbitrary block states or autonomous AI world mutation.

Sandbox remains experimental, manual and operator-only.
